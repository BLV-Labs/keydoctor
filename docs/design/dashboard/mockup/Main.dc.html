What Key Doctor does

Check keys

Reads every access key of an account, or of each council member, explains each key in one sentence, and scores the risk.

Clean up safely

A step-by-step plan you sign yourself in Meteor Wallet. Progress updates when you rescan. It never suggests deleting your last full-access key.

On Call

Compares your keys every day and messages you on Telegram when one changes. For a DAO, it also checks proposals every 15 minutes and warns the council before the final vote.
