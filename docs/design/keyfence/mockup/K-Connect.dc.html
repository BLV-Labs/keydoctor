How your agent works with Keyfence

Your agent asks; the fence decides; you answer only what is outside it.

Connect it

claude mcp add keyfence -- npx keyfence-agent --key ~/Downloads/research-bot.key.json

Then ask your agent: “What is my spending limit?”

What happens when it asks to pay

Paid at onceRecipient on your list, amount inside every limitShows in Activity
Waits for youRecipient not on your list, or over a limitNeeds you + Telegram, you approve or reject
RefusedBudget paused, agent turned off, too many waiting, or not enough balanceThe agent gets the reason

Tools your agent gets

guard_get_policyreads its limits and recipients
guard_request_transferasks for a payment, with a note
guard_get_request_statusfollows up on a waiting request

If you stop trusting it

  • Turn the agent off in its budget: it stops at once.
  • Pause all agents: 1 signature.
  • Remove the agent: its key stops working.
