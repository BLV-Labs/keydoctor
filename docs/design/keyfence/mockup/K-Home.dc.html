How it works

  1. Step 1

    Build the fence

    Pick a daily budget and a limit per payment, then list who the agent may pay.

  2. Step 2

    Connect your agent

    Your agent gets a key that can only ask for payments. One command connects it.

  3. Step 3

    Approve the rest

    Payments inside the fence go through. Anything else waits for your tap in Keyfence or Telegram.
