The only pool near a real price (57) is too thin: 5 NEAR gets 10.77 USDT against 5.67 for 1 NEAR, so each NEAR of a 5 NEAR run fetches 62% less than the first.

Anyone can trade or add liquidity on testnet pools, so any pool, ours included, can be moved between runs. `min_out` in the swap is what limits the loss (§11.11.3).

## Options for the demo

1. **Our own pool** on `ref-finance-101.testnet`: mint test USDT, add a new `SIMPLE_POOL` wNEAR/USDT seeded at a realistic price with enough depth that 5 NEAR moves the price by a few percent, and use its `pool_id`. Needs signing and testnet NEAR, so it is an owner step.
2. **Pool 57 with small runs** (for example 0.1 NEAR): no setup, but the price is thin and anyone can move it.
3. **A deep pool with a broken price** (118): the swap works, the numbers in Telegram and the video do not make sense.
